I am a Machine Learning Engineer and AI Safety Researcher based in Austin, TX. I currently work as a Data Engineer at Rivian Systems, building automated ETL pipelines and data quality systems on AWS (Glue, S3, Redshift, QuickSight, CloudWatch).

My research background is in AI safety and mechanistic interpretability of vision-language models: fine-tuning Gemma 3 4B IT VLMs on Nvidia A100 GPUs to study emergent misalignment, reproducing latent-intervention (direction steering) methods on text representations, and evaluating safety effects on MMSafetyBench with LLM-as-a-judge and human review, using vLLM for high-throughput inference. An anonymous manuscript from this work was accepted to the InterpScience Workshop at NeurIPS.

Previously I worked as a Data Scientist at TT Technologies (customer segmentation, forecasting), and as a Graduate Research Assistant at Colorado State University, applying machine learning (SVM, Random Forest, MLP, ResNet50) to hyperspectral imagery for early plant-disease detection.

I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from Colorado State University (2024) and am an AWS Certified Machine Learning Engineer.

> I want to study how advanced AI systems fail, how those failures can be detected through careful evaluation and interpretability methods, and how stronger defenses and control mechanisms can be designed in response.
