## Research Statement

> I want to study how advanced AI systems fail, how those failures can be detected through careful evaluation and interpretability methods, and how stronger defenses and control mechanisms can be designed in response.

## Current Directions

- **Mechanistic interpretability of VLMs** — comparing text- and image-conditioned representations; causal attribution of misaligned behavior to text-direction interventions rather than vision directions.
- **Safety evaluation** — fine-tuning Gemma 3 4B IT vision-language models to study emergent misalignment; MMSafetyBench with LLM-as-a-judge and human review; high-throughput evaluation with vLLM.
- **Robust ML under shift** — adversarial edge-case testing, class-imbalance and OOD weaknesses in applied models; hyperspectral disease detection (leaf-to-canopy scaling, modality-aware architectures).
- **Detection from real-world signals** — remote photoplethysmography from facial RGB video; PCA/clustering on noisy high-dimensional sensor data.
